Pittsburgh, 1996. Jessica Greer survived a media scandal, now she’s turning it into a crisis communications business. But can she handle the repercussions?

When star professor Richard Holiday is accused of sexually assaulting a freshman, the university hires Jessica to stop the story before it destroys their multimillion-dollar fundraising campaign. Her best friend and business partner, Sofia, wants no part of it. But Jessica forges ahead, believing they can do some good.

Jessica soon proves that when it comes to making ugly stories disappear, she’s very good.

Maybe too good.

When evidence appears to clear Richard, the university declares victory. And the public turns on the victim.

Then she dies.

Now Jessica is determined to tear apart the story she helped sell.

Anonymous Pittsburgh chat rooms buzz with gossip. A television reporter is digging for the story of her career. And Jessica begins to suspect that absolving Richard didn’t fix the problem, only buried the part that mattered.

But if Richard isn’t the real story, who is?
