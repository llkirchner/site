About the Book

The university hired her to make a university scandal disappear. They’ll regret it.

Pittsburgh, 1996. After a college freshman accuses her professor of sexual assault, the university hires Jessica Greer to control the damage.

When Jessica finds evidence clearing the professor, St. Augie’s University declares victory.

Then his accuser turns up dead.

Jessica knows something is terribly wrong with this story, and she won’t stop until she finds out what really happened.

A new psychological suspense from bestselling author L.L. Kirchner. Coming November 19.
