About the Book

Dangerous secrets unite two women across generations in this immersive historical saga.

Havana, 1950s. Thelma Miles runs the most exclusive nightclub in pre-Revolution Cuba, carving out a precarious independence. But when her reporter friend dies in a suspicious crash on the eve of publishing a high-stakes exposé, Thelma is left holding secrets worth killing to protect.

Miami, 1978. Sam Fontana, freshly divorced and adrift, discovers a sealed envelope left behind by her late mother—reporter Imogene Fuchs, who also died in a mysterious car “accident.” As Sam follows the clues Imogene left behind, she uncovers a chilling web of conspiracies stretching from glittering Havana nightclubs to the seedy underworld of 1970s Miami.

When Sam and Thelma’s paths collide, they face down the same powerful forces—organized crime, government cover-ups, and a family legacy of betrayal. To expose the truth, these two women must risk everything before those who silenced Imogene come back to finish the job.

Book 3 of The Queenpin Chronicles can be read as a gripping standalone or as the thrilling conclusion to the series.

For fans of The Alice Network and The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo.
