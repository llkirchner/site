About the Book

Pittsburgh, 1995. Ricin in the mail. A stolen byline. The FBI at her door. A blackout she can’t explain.

Jessica Greer, PR executive by day and alt-newsweekly critic by night, just lost her column and she wants it back. Instead she gets a poisoned rival, a federal case, and a headline she never asked for: The Mail Murderess.

For readers of Gillian Flynn, Shari Lapena, and Megan Abbott.
