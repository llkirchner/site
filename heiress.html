About the Book

Pittsburgh, January 1997. Jessica Greer has just refused to sign her name to one more convenient story. Then Bubsy Carmichael walks into her Butler Street office without an appointment.

Bubsy’s daughter Cat — newlywed, notorious party girl, heir to what’s left of an industrial fortune — is gone. So is Madison, the sober minder paid to keep Cat out of the papers. The one who started wearing Cat’s clothes. Styling her hair the same way. Borrowing her expressions.

The police say the two women left on a bender. The agency agrees. But Cat’s boyfriend is dead, the rumors are multiplying, and Bubsy has until the weekend before a search becomes another Carmichael scandal.

Jessica runs crisis communications, not missing-persons cases. She knows how a story gets built. And somebody is already building this one — around two women nobody can tell apart.

For fans of Gillian Flynn, Shari Lapena, and Megan Abbott.
